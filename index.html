About

I'm Pavan, a 3D designer working primarily in Blender. I take a piece from blockout to final frame: modelling, UV layout, PBR texturing, lighting, and compositing.

Most of my work sits between product and character — clean studio setups for objects, and stylised, readable shapes for characters and props. I also handle motion and edit, so a still can become a short loop or a full spot without leaving the pipeline.
